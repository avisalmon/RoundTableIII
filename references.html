References and External Source Notes

This page lists the external references and frameworks the book draws on, and records where each one is used. Entries marked as internal project materials are third-party documents the project does not hold publication rights to, so they are named but not distributed.

Core External Frameworks

OECD Teaching Compass

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Teacher agency.
  • Teacher professional identity.
  • Teacher competencies.
  • Teacher well-being.
  • The link between teacher development and learner agency.
  • Reimagining teachers as agents of curriculum change.

Source status:

  • Local tracked source: OECD Teaching Compass.

OECD Learning Compass 2030

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Future-oriented learning.
  • Student agency.
  • Knowledge, skills, attitudes, and values.
  • Transformative competencies.
  • Anticipation, action, and reflection.

Source status:

  • Web source: OECD Learning Compass 2030.

TALIS 2024

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Teacher professional context.
  • Teaching conditions.
  • Professional development background.
  • International comparison.

Source status:

  • Local tracked source: TALIS 2024 short material.

UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Teacher professional learning in AI-rich environments.

  • Web source: UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers.

Research References Listed in the Round Table Materials

Cognitive Apprenticeship

Collins, Brown, and Holum are referenced in the Meeting 3 discussion document.

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Learning through expert modeling.
  • Making thinking visible.
  • Teacher modeling of reasoning and judgment.

Self-Efficacy

Bandura is referenced in the Meeting 3 discussion document.

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Learner agency.
  • Belief in capability.
  • Teacher and student confidence in new learning environments.

Transfer of Learning

Perkins and Salomon are referenced in the Meeting 3 discussion document.

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Transfer of competencies across contexts.
  • Risks of teaching skills separately from disciplinary practice.

Moral Development in the Professions

Rest and Narvaez are referenced in the Meeting 3 discussion document.

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Ethical judgment.
  • Professional responsibility.
  • Moral reasoning in AI-supported learning and work.

Critical Thinking Instruction

Abrami and colleagues are referenced in the Meeting 3 discussion document.

Used in the manuscript for:

  • Teaching critical thinking explicitly.
  • Designing learning that goes beyond output quality.
